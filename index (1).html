A bit about me

I’m Tshepo Robert Mashilo, born and raised in Rietfontein, Verena, Mpumalanga, before moving to Mabopane, Pretoria.

I’m passionate about technology, creativity, spirituality and personal growth. I love travelling, exploring cultures and connecting with people.

I’ve travelled across all nine provinces of South Africa and internationally to Germany, Botswana and Eswatini, and I embrace every opportunity to learn, grow and make an impact. I’m also fluent in nearly eight languages.

My journey

The milestones, experiences and goals that shape where I'm headed.

  1. Where it started

    Marhagi Secondary School

    I attended Marhagi Secondary School and passed matric with a bachelor’s pass, which opened the door to higher education.

  2. 2018 – 2020

    Into the world of IT

    I went to Tshwane University of Technology to study Information Technology. I completed my Higher National Diploma and specialised in business analysis.

  3. 2021 – 2022

    My first real work

    I started out as a business analyst at MOEPI Publishing, where I also worked on project management. This is where what I had studied began to meet real work.

  4. 2022 – 2023

    Building on every side

    I went back to Tshwane University of Technology for my Advanced Diploma in Information Technology (Informatics), and kept working while I studied. I stepped into business development at Innovation Hub and Ecolabs in Pretoria, and spent time in data analysis with Deviare.

  5. 2023 – 2024

    Learning SAP

    I joined Netcampus Group as an SAP student and began building my skills in SAP, the systems that big businesses run on.

  6. 2024 – 2026

    Taking it global

    I worked as a Junior SAP Business Analyst at Mercedes-Benz AG, bringing my business analysis and SAP skills into a global company.

  7. 2026

    Into supply chain

    At Eskom, I applied SAP to warehouse and line supply, learning how the systems behind the scenes keep things moving.

  8. Now

    Moving into the cloud

    I am now an Azure Administrator at Mecer Inter-Ed. I work with Azure to manage user identities and access, deploy and automate infrastructure, monitor performance and costs, and set up backup and recovery.
